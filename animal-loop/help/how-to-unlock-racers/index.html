How do you unlock every racer in Animal Loop?

Animal Loop › How to unlock every racer

Published October 29, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

The short answer

Racers join your crew by playing. Reach a platinum medal in the right achievement, beat a world's rival in its Grand Prix, race on 15 days in November for Turkey, or share and rate the game for Otter and Horsefish. The Hedgehog is with you from the start. If you'd rather not wait, any earnable racer can be unlocked early for $0.99, or every racer you can buy for $4.99.

Frequently asked questions

How much do racers cost?

Nothing, if you'd rather play for them: every racer can be earned in the game. If you don't want to wait, you can unlock any earnable racer early for $0.99, or get every racer you can buy for $4.99 with the Unlock All Racers bundle. Otter and Horsefish can only be earned.

How do medals unlock racers?

Every achievement has four medals: bronze, silver, gold and platinum. Reach platinum in the achievement linked to a racer and that racer joins your crew. Your progress toward each medal shows on the Achievements page.

When can I get Turkey?

Turkey comes around in November. Race on 15 different days in November and it joins your crew. Any race counts, one a day, and your days add up every year, so you can finish next November if you miss some. You can also unlock it early for $0.99.

Can I buy Otter or Horsefish?

No. Otter joins your crew when you send the game to a friend, and Horsefish when you rate the game. Neither is in the bundle.

Which racer do I start with?

The Hedgehog. It's free from the start, and every other racer joins along the way.
