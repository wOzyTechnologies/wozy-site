One tap to steer

No stick, no brake. Your animal curls up and rolls in a straight line, and each tap turns it. Time your taps to stay off the walls and keep your hearts.

  • ♥♥♥♥♥5 hearts a trackEvery wall you bump costs one, so a clean line feels great.
  • ★★★Up to 3 starsSurvive 1, 3 and 5 laps to earn them. The road speeds up every lap.
  • →One lap opens the nextFinish a lap and the next track is yours. Star every track to open the Grand Prix.

Three worlds, dawn to night

Nine handmade tracks in every world, from simple loops to knots that cross over themselves. Switch gates and bridges show up everywhere, and every new world brings a new twist.

World 1Dune Flats

Desert roads past the pyramids, where switch gates flip your turns and figure-8s zip over bridges.

World 2Coral Shallows

Sandbars over turquoise water, where sticky mud shows up to slow you down.

World 3Fern Hollow

Forest trails where moles dig you a shortcut, if you can bonk them first.

BridgesZip over bridges and dive through tunnels.
Switch gatesCross a gate and your turns flip. Keep your thumb on its toes.
MudSkim the edge of the mud, or get stuck in the squelch.
Mole tunnelsBonk the mole, then roll down its hole and pop out ahead.
WallsEvery bump costs a heart. Hit one too often and it breaks apart: sand, stone or wood.

How racers join your crew

  • Earn medalsEvery achievement has bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals. Reach platinum and a new racer rolls in.
  • Beat the rivalWin a world's Grand Prix faster than its rival, and the Armadillo, Puffer or Ladybug joins you.
  • Race in NovemberTurkey only comes around once a year. Race on 15 days in November and it's yours.
  • Share the loveSend the game to a friend and Otter tags along. Rate the game and Horsefish drifts in.

Seasonal update

A new look every season

The title screen dresses up for the time of year where you are, and flips when you're south of the equator.

  • ❄️ Winter snow
  • 🌸 Spring petals
  • ✨ Summer fireflies
  • 🍂 Autumn leaves

Turkey, the first seasonal racer, only comes around in November. Race on 15 different days that month and it joins your crew. Miss a few? Your days carry over to next November.

Take on the Grand Prix

Every world ends with one: all nine tracks, back to back, at the speed you pick, against that world's rival animal. Star every track in a world to open its Grand Prix.

  • Start on Beginner and you get unlimited lives, so anyone can make it to the finish.
  • Push your limit with Up to 250cc, then No limit, which keeps rising as you get faster.
  • Save & Leave mid-race and pick it up later, with your time and lives, on the same track.
  • Every finish gets a ceremony: beat the rival and it joins your crew, lose and it gloats, and once it's yours, you take the podium.
  • Race your friends: once you've finished a Grand Prix, your Game Center friends join you there as ghosts, at the pace of their best times.

Race into the night

Every track has its own day. Race from sunrise into the night as the sun moves across the sky, and watch your shadow follow it.

Every world has its own sound

All the music was made for Animal Loop. Each world plays the songs on its own instruments, so the desert, the sea and the forest each sound like a different place. And the faster you roll, the faster it plays.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Animal Loop?

Your animal curls up and rolls forward on its own, and every tap turns it. There's no stick and no brake: time your taps to stay off the walls and keep your hearts.

What is a Grand Prix?

The big race at the end of every world: all nine of the world's tracks, raced back to back at one speed you pick, against that world's rival animal. Beat the rival and they join your crew.

Read the full answer →

How do you play Animal Loop with friends?

Through Game Center. Tap + on the Friends page to send a friend the game and a Game Center friend request. Once you're Game Center friends, their Grand Prix times show up next to yours, and in any Grand Prix you've finished, they race with you as ghosts.

Read the full answer →

Is Animal Loop free?

Yes. Animal Loop is free on the App Store, and every racer you can buy can also be earned by playing. In-app purchases are optional.

How much do racers cost?

Nothing, if you'd rather play for them: every racer can be earned in the game. If you don't want to wait, you can unlock any earnable racer early for $0.99, or get every racer you can buy for $4.99 with the Unlock All Racers bundle. Otter and Horsefish can only be earned.

Which devices can I play Animal Loop on?

iPhone and iPad, including iPhone Duo, plus Apple silicon Macs, where it runs as the iPad app. One tap still steers, whatever you're holding.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or later. On Mac, Animal Loop runs as the iPad app on Apple silicon Macs.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes. Your progress syncs through iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac, and it comes back if you reinstall the game.

How many tracks and racers are there?

27 handmade tracks across three worlds, Dune Flats, Coral Shallows and Fern Hollow, and 19 animal racers to collect, from a hamster to a turkey.

How do I unlock new racers?

Earn platinum medals in achievements, beat a world's rival in its Grand Prix, race on 15 days in November for Turkey, or share and rate the game for Otter and Horsefish.

See every racer and how to unlock it →

When can I get Turkey?

Turkey comes around in November. Race on 15 different days in November and it joins your crew. Any race counts, one a day, and your days add up every year, so you can finish next November if you miss some. You can also unlock it early for $0.99.

Meet Turkey →

How is Animal Loop different from other racing games?

Most one-tap games are endless runs for a high score. Animal Loop has real, handmade tracks to master, rivals to beat and friends to race, with a single tap as your only control.
