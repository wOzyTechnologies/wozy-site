SHORT DESCRIPTION
Animal Loop is a one-tap racing game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your animal
rolls on its own, and every tap turns it. Race 18 animals across 27 handmade
tracks in three worlds, take on a Grand Prix in every world, and race into the
night as the sun sets on every track.

LONG DESCRIPTION
In Animal Loop there is no stick and no brake. Your animal curls up and rolls
forward in a straight line, and each tap turns it. Time your taps to stay off
the walls and keep your hearts.

The game spans three worlds, each with nine handmade tracks, from simple loops
to knots that cross over and under themselves. Dune Flats adds switch gates
that flip which way a tap turns you. Coral Shallows adds bridges and loops that
pass back under themselves. Fern Hollow adds sticky mud and moles that dig you
a shortcut.

Every world ends with a Grand Prix: all nine tracks back to back, at the speed
you pick, against that world's rival animal. Win it and the rival joins your
roster. Game Center friends race alongside you as ghosts of their best runs.
Every track also has its own day/night cycle, with shadows that follow the sun.

WHY IT'S FUN TO PLAY
- One tap. Easy to pick up, hard to master. Your animal rolls on its own and
  every tap turns it. Anyone can play in seconds. Holding a clean line at top
  speed takes real skill.
- Three worlds that each play differently: switch gates in Dune Flats, bridges
  and tunnels in Coral Shallows, sticky mud and mole shortcuts in Fern Hollow,
  and walls that crack if you keep hitting them. 27 handmade tracks, and no two
  ride the same.
- Race your friends, no setup: Game Center friends' best runs show up on the
  track as ghosts. No invites and no waiting.
- Prove you're the best in the Grand Prix: all nine tracks of a world back to
  back against its rival animal. Win, and the rival joins your crew.
- Plenty to unlock: 18 animals to collect and achievements to earn.
  Everything in the game can be unlocked by playing.
- Play anywhere: progress syncs through iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  On iPhone Duo it plays folded or open, however you hold it.
- Free, and always growing: new tracks, worlds and racers are added regularly.
  Next up: Frost Valley, a fourth world where the ice breaks behind you.

THE STORY BEHIND IT
- Sixteen years from sketch to App Store: Animal Loop began in 2010 as
  "Hamster Racing", a pen sketch made right after wOzy launched its first
  game, TicToe Fury, for the first iPad. It was shelved. Development restarted
  from a blank Xcode project on July 28, 2026, and Sebastián Salazar de
  Greiff built the whole game on his own.
- Every animal is drawn in code: no image files for the art. Every animal,
  plant and landmark is built from code shapes, so the whole scene recolors
  live from day to night, at up to 120 fps.
- Original music that changes with each world: all the music was made for the
  game and is played live, shifting its sound to match the world.
- A bot that tests every track: with one button, a track that's slightly too
  narrow can become impossible. So Sebastián built a level editor, a track
  analyzer that scores each track's difficulty, and a bot that plays every
  track with human-like mistakes to find where it breaks. Every track is rated
  from 150cc to 285cc.
