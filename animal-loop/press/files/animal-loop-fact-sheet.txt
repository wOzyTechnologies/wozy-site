SHORT DESCRIPTION
Animal Loop is a one-tap racing game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your animal
rolls on its own, and every tap turns it. Race 19 animals across 27 handmade
tracks in three worlds, take on a Grand Prix in every world, and race into the
night as the sun sets on every track.

LONG DESCRIPTION
In Animal Loop there is no stick and no brake. Your animal curls up and rolls
forward in a straight line, and each tap turns it. Time your taps to stay off
the walls and keep your hearts.

The game spans three worlds, each with nine handmade tracks, from simple loops
to knots that cross over and under themselves. Dune Flats brings switch gates
that flip which way a tap turns you, and bridges where the track passes back
under itself. Both show up in every world after that. Coral Shallows adds
sticky mud. Fern Hollow adds moles that dig you a shortcut.

Every world ends with a Grand Prix: all nine tracks back to back, at the speed
you pick, against that world's rival animal. Win it and the rival joins your
crew. Once you've finished a Grand Prix, your Game Center friends race it with
you as ghosts. Every track also has its own day/night cycle, with shadows that
follow the sun.

THE WORLDS
- Dune Flats (World 1). Rival: Armadillo. Brings switch gates, plus bridges
  and tunnels. Tracks: Oasis, Canteen, Rattler, Saddle, Hourglass, Lasso,
  Tumbleweed, Gourd, Scorpion.
- Coral Shallows (World 2). Rival: Puffer. Brings sticky mud. Tracks: Bubble,
  Teardrop, Shield, Clover, Cove, Keyhole, Trefoil Knot, Totem, Anchor.
- Fern Hollow (World 3). Rival: Ladybug. Brings moles that dig you a shortcut.
  Tracks: Acorn, Leaf, Plectrum, Mitten, Horseshoe, Mushroom, Bunny, Tooth,
  Ivy Leaf.

WHY IT'S FUN TO PLAY
- One tap. Easy to pick up, hard to master. Your animal rolls on its own and
  every tap turns it. Anyone can play in seconds. Holding a clean line at top
  speed takes real skill.
- Three worlds that each play differently: switch gates and bridges from the
  very first world, sticky mud in Coral Shallows, mole shortcuts in Fern
  Hollow, and walls of sand, stone or wood that break apart if you keep
  hitting them. 27 handmade tracks, and no two ride the same.
- Race your friends' ghosts: once you've finished a world's Grand Prix, your
  Game Center friends who've raced it join you as ghosts, at the pace of their
  best times. Pass them, then brag about it.
- Prove you're the best in the Grand Prix: all nine tracks of a world back to
  back against its rival animal. Win, and the rival joins your crew.
- Plenty to unlock: 19 animals to collect, including Turkey, the first
  seasonal racer, and achievements to earn. Every racer you can buy can also
  be earned by playing.
- Play anywhere: progress syncs through iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  On iPhone Duo it plays folded or open, however you hold it.
- Free, and always growing: the whole game is free to download, and it keeps
  growing. Turkey, the first seasonal racer, joined in the seasonal update.

THE STORY BEHIND IT
- Sixteen years from sketch to App Store: Animal Loop began in 2010 as
  "Hamster Racing", a pen sketch made right after wOzy launched its first
  game, TicToe Fury, for the first iPad. It was shelved. Development restarted
  from scratch on July 28, 2026, and Sebastián Salazar de Greiff built the
  whole game on his own.
- A world that changes color with the sun: nothing in Animal Loop is a flat
  picture. The game draws every animal, tree and landmark itself, live, so the
  whole world shifts color with the sun, from golden mornings to moonlit nights.
- Every world has its own sound: all the music was made for the game. Each
  world plays the songs on its own instruments, so the desert, the sea and the
  forest each sound like a different place, and the music speeds up as you do.
