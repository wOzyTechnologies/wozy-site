About the game

Short description

Animal Loop is a one-tap racing game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your animal rolls on its own, and every tap turns it. Race 19 animals across 27 handmade tracks in three worlds, take on a Grand Prix in every world, and race into the night as the sun sets on every track.

Long description

In Animal Loop there is no stick and no brake. Your animal curls up and rolls forward in a straight line, and each tap turns it. Time your taps to stay off the walls and keep your hearts.

The game spans three worlds, each with nine handmade tracks, from simple loops to knots that cross over and under themselves. Dune Flats brings switch gates that flip which way a tap turns you, and bridges where the track passes back under itself. Both show up in every world after that. Coral Shallows adds sticky mud. Fern Hollow adds moles that dig you a shortcut.

Every world ends with a Grand Prix: all nine tracks back to back, at the speed you pick, against that world's rival animal. Win it and the rival joins your crew. Once you've finished a Grand Prix, your Game Center friends race it with you as ghosts. Every track also has its own day/night cycle, with shadows that follow the sun.

Why it's fun to play

  • One tap. Easy to pick up, hard to master.

    Your animal rolls on its own and every tap turns it. Anyone can play in seconds. Holding a clean line at top speed takes real skill.

  • Three worlds that each play differently

    Switch gates and bridges from the very first world, sticky mud in Coral Shallows, mole shortcuts in Fern Hollow, and walls of sand, stone or wood that break apart if you keep hitting them. 27 handmade tracks, and no two ride the same.

  • Race your friends' ghosts

    Once you've finished a world's Grand Prix, your Game Center friends who've raced it join you as ghosts, at the pace of their best times. Pass them, then brag about it.

  • Prove you're the best in the Grand Prix

    Race all nine tracks of a world back to back against its rival animal. Win, and the rival joins your crew.

  • Plenty to unlock

    Collect 19 animals, including Turkey, the first seasonal racer, and earn achievements along the way. Every racer you can buy can also be earned by playing.

  • Play anywhere

    Your progress syncs through iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac. On iPhone Duo it plays folded or open, however you hold it.

  • Free, and always growing

    The whole game is free to download, and it keeps growing: Turkey, the first seasonal racer, joined in the seasonal update.

The story behind it

  • Sixteen years from sketch to App Store

    Animal Loop began in 2010 as Hamster Racing, a pen sketch made right after wOzy launched its first game, TicToe Fury, for the first iPad. It was shelved. Development finally restarted from scratch on July 28, 2026, and Sebastián Salazar de Greiff, co-founder of wOzy, built the whole game on his own.

  • A world that changes color with the sun

    Nothing in Animal Loop is a flat picture. The game draws every animal, tree and landmark itself, live, so the whole world shifts color with the sun, from golden mornings to moonlit nights.

  • Every world has its own sound

    All the music was made for the game. Each world plays the songs on its own instruments, so the desert, the sea and the forest each sound like a different place, and the music speeds up as you do.

The full story is in the dev diary: One Tap, Twenty-Seven Tracks, and a Simulation Engine I Never Planned to Build.
